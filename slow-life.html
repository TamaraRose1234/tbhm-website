We were never meant to go this hard…

Our bodies were never designed to be this connected: a phone in our hand from the moment we wake up, a ping for every notification and instant access to us from everyone, so we're taking in everyone else's needs all day long. On top of that, nobody ever taught us how to slow down in the middle of this overwhelm; instead, we were encouraged to keep going, keep producing and push through everything we're dealing with.

Most of us run on adrenaline and extra coffees all day, scroll until midnight and wake up already behind. More technology means more productivity, more connection and an expectation to move at a pace that honestly isn't good for us.

That's exactly why this training exists. Living the Slow Life is your chance to learn how to slow down in the middle of all of it, and to discover how to regulate your nervous system so that no matter what the world is doing around you, you can stay calm, connected, present and in control of your reality.

It's one of the fundamental trainings all of my clients go through, and it's mandatory, because the women who make the biggest leaps are the ones who have learned how to manage their reactions, stay calm and present no matter what's happening, and live in a new structure that feeds that calm and regulates their nervous system. Nothing else can change until your foundation is running on that calm, steady field.

The best part is that you don't have to do 30 things at once to overhaul your life. It's one small practice every day that changes something in your home or your routines, until you eat, live and breathe a slower paced life.

What you'll practice…

Your evenings

A bath or shower before bed, softer lights, pre-sleep intentions, an evening playlist, calming scents, no screens the hour before bed, reading instead of scrolling, a cup of tea and going to sleep when you're tired.

Your mornings

Water first thing, making your bed, journaling, waking up intentionally instead of reaching for your phone, a morning stretch by the window and the first days of a meditation practice.

Your home and your body

Leaving your shoes at the door, upgrading your sleep zone, decluttering, a caffeine cutoff, breath breaks, barefoot time in nature and loving on yourself.
