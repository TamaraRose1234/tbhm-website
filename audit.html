Before you book…

Who is the Audit for?

If you're in the middle of a big transition, you kind of know what you need to do, but you're still hesitant, can't seem to do it and aren't sure how to move forward, this could be for you. If you're a founder or business owner who keeps stalling in the same place, this could be for you. And if you've done some therapy, coaching or courses but can't seem to pinpoint why things aren't changing, this could be for you.

Is this a sales call?

No. The Audit is a full energetic session dedicated to you. You'll want to keep the recording, go back and rewatch the session, read the transcript and review your Energetic Blueprint long after the call. If you want to talk about next steps, we book a separate time for that, and only after you've had your Audit.

What does "credited in full" mean?

If you join the Divine Self Mastery program, work with me privately or take any course within our library, your investment for any of those is reduced by $497.

I bought Rise of the Divine with the Audit add-on. Do I book here?

Your Audit is already paid. Use the booking link in your Rise welcome email, or reply to it and we'll send it again.

What if I'm not ready?

Get to know the work first. Watch the playlists on our YouTube channel, starting with "Start Here: Why You're Still Stuck," and listen to the long conversations on The Next Evolution podcast. The Audit will be here when the timing is right.

How do I prepare for the session?

Choose a quiet, private space with a good internet connection, and give yourself 90 minutes or more. Come to the call a few minutes early and center yourself, make sure you won't be interrupted, and use headphones if they're appropriate for your space.
