You don't need to be good at this…

Most of us were never taught how to slow down. We were taught to keep going, keep producing and push through, so when we finally sit still, the mind races, the body fidgets and it feels like we're doing it wrong.

You're not doing it wrong. Your nervous system has been running on high for a long time, and it needs a gentle, steady way back. That's what these 20 days are for: a little more each day, a new practice each day, until you find the ones that feel like coming home.

How it works…

1

Watch the short training

Each day opens with a short video (also as audio) where I show you that day's practice and why it works.

2

Meditate, 5 minutes to 20

Day 1 is 5 minutes, and each day adds a minute, so by Day 20 you're sitting for 20 minutes without forcing it.

3

Journal before and after

Your 20-Day Journal gives you a few prompts for each sit, so you can see what's shifting in your thoughts, your feelings and your body.

4

Check in on Day 10 and Day 20

Two short check-ins show you how far you've come, and the meditation-only playlist lets you return to any practice whenever you need it.

What you'll practice…

Days 1–5 · Coming back to yourself

Presence and breath, letting thoughts pass without following them, letting go of distractions, a Vipassana body scan and a practice for letting fear dissolve.

Days 6–13 · Your energy

Chakras and healing, sound bowls, meditating with crystals, money and abundance, connecting with your infinite being, the energies of Lakshmi and Ganesha, and pure silence.

Days 14–20 · The Seven Sacred Flames

One flame a day, from Illumination and Wisdom to the Violet Flame, each matched to a day of the week so you can keep returning to it.

The one rule

Cultivate your awareness without judgment…

Even if you miss a day, your number one opportunity is to not judge yourself. This is about building a muscle and training a new habit, and every time you come back, you're rewiring the patterns that create the life you actually want.

Yes. The series starts with 5 minutes and builds slowly to 20, and every day begins with a short training that shows you exactly what to do.

What if I miss a day?

Pick up where you left off, without judging yourself. The practice is the coming back.

Is it really free?

No. You'll try practices from several traditions, from breath and body scans to sound and silence, and keep what works for you.
