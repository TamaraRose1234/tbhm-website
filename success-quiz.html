Feminine archetype quiz questions, answered

What are the feminine archetypes?

Feminine archetypes are recurring patterns in how women lead, create, connect and protect themselves. This quiz uses four: the Sovereign Hustler, the Visionary Priestess, the Shape-Shifting Muse and the Hidden Oracle, each describing how you get things done and the pattern that tends to drain you.

How many feminine archetypes are there?

It depends on the system; some use four, some seven, some twelve. My quiz uses four archetypes built around how you work, lead and burn out, because those are the patterns I see most in high-achieving women.
