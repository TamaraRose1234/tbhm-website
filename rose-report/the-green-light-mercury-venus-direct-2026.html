Germany Is Set to Elect Its First Woman President

Germany has had twelve presidents, and every one has been a man. On Monday, Chancellor Friedrich Merz nominated Ilse Aigner, the president of Bavaria's state parliament, to be the next one. She's a conservative from his side, a member of the CSU, the Bavarian sister party of his Christian Democrats. Merz's conservatives and their partners in the governing coalition, the Social Democrats, back her, and the Greens' leadership is asking its members to back her too, so she's very likely to be elected when the Federal Convention votes on Saturday, January 30, 2027.

Standing next to Merz on Monday, she said she wants to bring a divided country back together and reach the people who feel left on the sidelines… “I don’t want to stand above the citizens, I’m one of them.”

Here in the United States, we just watched woman after woman step into seats no woman had held before… and the wave keeps moving around the world.

Be sure to care for your nervous system this week, and don't hold on to any energy that's not actually yours or that isn't going to serve you.

Catherine Breed Swam the Entire California Coast… 900 Miles in 111 Days

Catherine Breed, 33, from Marin County, started at the Oregon border in July and swam south, day after day, through storms, big waves and sharks, with her crew and two boats beside her. On Monday, October 19, she walked out of the water at Coronado, near the Mexico border, 111 days and nearly 900 miles later.

Did she break any records? Yes… she's the first person on record to swim the entire California coast, and she believes she's the first to even try it. It isn't her first record, either. The former Cal swimmer holds nine open water records, and in 2022 she became the first person to swim from the Golden Gate Bridge to Half Moon Bay.

What had her do it? She grew up on this coastline and loves the ocean. She swam to raise money and awareness for Sea Dreamers, the nonprofit she founded to help people, women especially, get out on the ocean and take care of it, and to show women and girls they can go after the unlikely, even the impossible.

How did she keep going for 111 days? In her words, "When I get in the ocean, it's a full surrender."
