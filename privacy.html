Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The Beyond Human Method Inc ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit thebeyondhumanmethod.com or use our programs, how we use it and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number and anything you share in intake forms, reflections, emails or sessions.
  • Payment information: processed securely by our payment providers (such as Stripe and Circle). We don't store full card numbers.
  • Usage information: pages visited, links clicked, device and browser type and approximate location, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To deliver sessions, programs and the community you've signed up for.
  • To send emails you've asked for, including session reminders and our newsletter. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.
  • To understand how our website is used and make it better.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We never sell your personal information.

Session content and confidentiality

What you share in sessions, intake forms and reflections is treated as confidential. Sessions may be recorded so you can revisit them; recordings are shared with you and stored securely. We'll never use your story, name or likeness publicly without your written permission.

Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to make the site work and analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand which pages are helpful. Analytics data is aggregated and doesn't identify you by name. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the business, under their own privacy commitments: scheduling (Calendly), video (Zoom), community and courses (Circle), email (Mailchimp), payments (Stripe), contracts (DocuSign), forms (Tally) and analytics (Google). We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you're a client or subscriber, and afterward only as long as needed for legal, tax and accounting purposes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it for marketing. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado or the European Union), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them. Email connect@thebeyondhumanmethod.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the effective date above, and we'll let clients know about significant changes by email.
