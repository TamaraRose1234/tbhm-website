Everyone's life is running on a primary pattern… but the good news is it can be cleared.

The Beyond Human Method is a methodology built over the last 15 years, from more than 20,000 hours of guiding people through change. Our goal is to find that one energetic pattern at the root of all of your money, relationship, body, career and parenting issues. Then we clear it at the energetic root level, so your whole life moves forward at one time.

Most people work on the branches, but never tend to the root…

Most personal growth and development is organized by topic. You may have a business coach for your business, a therapist for past traumas, a trainer for your body and a book or a podcast guru for your money. Each one makes sense on its own, but they all work at the surface level.

Think of yourself as a tree. You keep trimming back the branches without ever tending to the root, and the truth is, your pattern doesn't live in any of the branches or in one area; it's in the root of your tree. That's why, when you work on one area with a specialist, it can be hard to make the big change you want, because you haven't focused any attention on the root.

That's why this methodology is so powerful and works for so many different types of people. When clients focus on the root pattern and energetically change it, their relationships, their money, their business success and their health and well-being all begin to shift together, positively, in the direction they want.

Think about it this way

You might have an energetic root pattern like "I'm not good enough." What you'll find is that you overproduce to prove your value and worth to others; you think you have to do more to receive love and be valued. In a world where value is measured by doing and not by being, all of that makes sense, and the cycle keeps feeding itself.

This pattern probably shows up in your bank account, in how much money you earn and the feeling that you have to work harder to have more. It shows up in your relationships, trying to do more in order to receive love. It shows up in how you parent, saying yes to everything so you can be the parent you think you're supposed to be. And in your business, success only ever seems to come from more hours, more effort and more of you.

How you apply the work

You'll focus on mastering three key practices until they become how you eat, live and breathe.

1

Find the root pattern.

Together, we'll identify the primary energetic root pattern that keeps you creating the same reality over and over again. Many people, once they find their root pattern, think the answer is to live in the opposite of it, so they never clear it. For example, if you have a root pattern of not good enough, you may overgive in your relationships to receive love. Somewhere early on, you learned that you have to do more to be loved and valued in your closest relationships, with your family and your partners. Once you realize that, your answer may be to put boundaries down, to pull back and to do less, and that looks like something you've healed, but all you've done is stay in the same energy and go the opposite direction.

2

Clear it, and create a new reality.

Once the pattern is cleared at the energetic level, which is the root and the work we do, it's released from the body, the nervous system and the energy field, and you stop living in the story you built your reality on. But you've already been patterned in the old reality, so clearing the pattern is only part of it. Your next step is to create the new reality, a life you genuinely love and are excited to live in, and that happens once the pattern has been identified and cleared.

3

Energetic alignment mastery.

Energy lives in your thoughts, feelings and actions. Once the first two steps are in place, it's your job to consistently match your energy to the life and reality you want. This may be the most uncomfortable part of the process, because your old reality is so familiar to you and your habits are built on those patterns and habitual ways of being. You'll know you're doing it right when things feel uncomfortable, because the discomfort is exactly where the change happens. You'll build a muscle to be comfortable with the discomfort, to choose new things and to step out of the old patterning, and that's why this program requires a full year commitment. I want you to be successful.

The five pillars you learn to master for yourself.

Every practice in the method sits under one of these five pillars, so that by the end of one full year, you're working with them without thinking about them, and you see your life change in front of you.

Clear

Release the patterns, beliefs and lineage you inherited, so they stop running your life.

Align

Bring your thoughts, feelings and actions into coherence with the life you really want to be living.

Create

Learn how to co-create with universal source energy, so you stop working so hard to force outcomes.

Intuit

Learn to hear your intuition clearly, tell it apart from fear and live in alignment with where it's guiding you.

Trust

Let go of your need to control and act on what you know is true for you; this is where life starts to feel easy.

Underneath all five

A new foundation of living.

You'll regulate your nervous system in a way you have yet to experience, with daily practices, new routines and soul-filled rituals that become your new way of living and being. When you do this, everything else falls into place with ease.
