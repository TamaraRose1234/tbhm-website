Book Tamara to speak

Relatable, intuitive and transformational, from the first five minutes.

Tamara Rose speaks to rooms full of high-achieving women who are doing everything right and still working far too hard for it. She makes energy practical, gets the room laughing at its own humanity and leaves people feeling something shift before they walk out the door.

She knows how to hold a room…

With more than 20,000 hours guiding women through life and business transitions, from her own live events to intimate private work, Tamara knows how to hold a room while real change happens in it. She has hosted multi-day events and retreats, virtual and in person, and guided thousands of women around the world, and before any of that she was the number one sales rep at her company, so she speaks the language of high performers and knows exactly what burnout costs on the other side of it.

Tamara can tailor any talk to any audience.

Here are a few of the topics past organizers have chosen…

Why Am I So Tired? Burnout in High-Achieving Women, and What Actually Changes It

It's not what you think, and there is an answer. Rest alone doesn't fix this kind of tired, and Tamara shows your audience why, what's really driving the exhaustion and what actually changes it. They leave able to name the pattern underneath it, with simple action steps they can start the same day.

Best for women's leadership conferences, wellness events and employee resource groups.

Is It Greedy to Want More? How to Want More Without Doing More

We've been taught that wanting more means doing more, and that couldn't be further from the truth. Tamara shows why wanting more isn't greedy, what sits underneath the guilt, and how to want more money, time and joy without adding more to an already full plate.

Best for women's networks, entrepreneur summits and women in leadership programs.

The Power of No: People Pleasing, Boundaries and Saying No Without Guilt

For the women who are praised for being so helpful while they're quietly drowning. A warm, funny and very direct look at why saying no can feel unsafe, how to let the nervous system get comfortable being unavailable, and what opens up when you stop overgiving. It includes Tamara's three-step NO framework, ready to use the same day.

Best for women's networks, employee resource groups and mothers' groups.

Run Your Life Like a President: Delegation, Decisions and Getting Your Time Back

A president doesn't answer every email. Tamara shows high-capacity women how to decide what only they can do, hand off the rest without guilt or overexplaining, and get real time back in the week, from someone who was the number one sales rep at her company before she built her own business.

Best for business owners, executive teams and leadership retreats.

Workshop: Trust Your Gut at Work, Intuition and Decision-Making for Women Leaders

How intuition actually shows up, how to tell it apart from fear, and a simple practice for making decisions you trust, in the meeting and in life. Interactive, 90 minutes to a half day.

In person or virtual, keynote to full day.

  • Keynote, 30 to 60 minutes, with a live experiential moment the room remembers.
  • Workshop, 90 minutes to half a day, with guided practice and Q&A, including Trust Your Gut at Work.
  • Panel or fireside chat, 20 to 45 minutes, as a panelist, a featured conversation or the moderator.
  • Retreat facilitation, a full day or a weekend, including clearings.
  • Podcasts and virtual summits, as a guest or a featured conversation.

For event organizers…

How do I check availability?

Email bookings@thebeyondhumanmethod.com with your event, date, audience and the format you have in mind, and someone from our booking team will get back to you personally, usually within a few days, to talk through fit, availability and next steps.

What are Tamara's fees?

Fees depend on the format, the length and any travel, so the booking team shares them once we know a little about your event.

Is there a speaker kit?

Yes. A short, medium and long bio, headshots and the talk descriptions are available on request; just ask in your inquiry.

Is there an introduction script?

Yes, and your host is welcome to read it as written:

Our next speaker has spent more than 15 years guiding women through the moments that change everything. Before that, Tamara Rose was the number one sales rep at her company, which is exactly where she learned what it costs to succeed inside the systems women were handed. Today she is the founder of The Beyond Human Method, where she helps high-achieving women find the pattern underneath what keeps them stuck and clear it at the energy level, so it stops repeating. She has hosted multi-day events and retreats, virtual and in person, and guided thousands of women around the world, and she's about to make you laugh, make you think and maybe feel something shift. Please welcome Tamara Rose.

Does Tamara travel?

Yes. She's based in the Rocky Mountains of Colorado and speaks in person across the US, and virtually anywhere.
