Where the show came from

Formerly Divine Feminine Radio, now the next chapter.

Divine Feminine Radio started it all: more than 50 episodes on energy, intuition and what changes when women stop living by someone else's rules, and the conversation kept getting bigger than its name.

Now the show is expanding, and The Next Evolution is where it goes from here: I'm in conversation with the people rewriting the systems women were handed, those living, building and leading in new ways, and the women whose rising power is changing what's possible for all of us.
