What Is Energy Clearing? A Plain-Language Answer

By Tamara RoseUpdated October 1, 20265 minute read

Energy clearing is the process of releasing a pattern's charge from your body and energy field, so the old reaction stops arising instead of being something you manage. If that sounds a little woo, stay with me, because in practice it's surprisingly grounded.

Start with what you already know

You already read energy all the time. You walk into a room and feel the tension before anyone speaks. You know within a minute whether a new person feels safe. Your body tightens before a hard conversation and softens with someone you love. None of that is mystical; it's information your system picks up and responds to, far faster than your thinking mind.

A pattern works the same way. It's not just a belief in your head. It's a charge your body carries, a familiar tightening or bracing that switches on in certain situations and drives your reactions before you've had a chance to think.

What clearing does

Clearing works with that charge directly. Rather than analyzing the story, we identify the pattern, locate where it lives in you and release it, so the situation that used to switch it on simply doesn't anymore. Clients often describe it as a physical sense of space, lightness or quiet where something used to be.

Then comes the part that makes it last: practicing a new way of thinking, feeling and acting that matches the life you actually want. Clearing creates the space, and alignment fills it with something new.

What a session is like

Sessions happen on Zoom or in person. You talk, I listen for the pattern, and then I guide you through the clearing while you stay awake, present and in charge. You might feel emotion move, warmth, tingling or a deep calm, and some people feel very little in the moment and notice the shift over the following days. Nothing is done to you; you're learning to do it yourself.

How it differs from what you've tried

  • Therapy helps you understand and process your story. Clearing works on the pattern's charge underneath the story, and the two work well side by side.
  • Coaching gives you strategy and accountability. Clearing removes what keeps pulling you away from the strategy.
  • Meditation calms your system, and it's part of the foundation. Clearing goes further and releases a specific pattern.

Is it for skeptics?

Some of my most committed clients are deeply analytical people, including a business owner with a master's degree in clinical psychology and several founders who wanted proof before anything else. You don't need to believe anything in advance. You only need to be willing to notice what changes. This isn't a replacement for medical or mental health care, and if you're in crisis, please contact a licensed professional first.
